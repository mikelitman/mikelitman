## How I work (added October 2026)

- I don't use the terminal. Never give me commands to paste. Do it yourself, or tell me exactly where to click in the Claude app, GitHub or the website.
- Short request from me? Use the `brief` skill first: write the goal and the "done when" checks, then start work.
- Before merging, deploying or saying "done" on anything non-trivial, use the `second-opinion` skill. Only bring me decisions about taste, money or anything public.
- Long sessions: when this session passes roughly 200k tokens of context, or switches to a different project, say so, run `session-save`, and suggest a fresh session. Don't wait until it's sluggish.
- Anything you schedule (crons, Actions, routines) must tell me when it fails. A job that fails silently counts as broken.
- New paid service or anything that deploys on a schedule: tell me what it costs per month and how to switch it off.
